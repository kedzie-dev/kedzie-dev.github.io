# kedzie-dev.github.io

A Korean technical blog about operating Kubernetes, GitOps, and SRE systems. The site is designed as a reading tool: simple navigation, readable articles, and accessible interactions.

## Content collections

- **Operational Notes** (`/notes/`): focused explanations of operational behavior, decisions, and trade-offs.
- **Incident Labs** (`/labs/`): interactive scenarios for inspecting signals and choosing responses.
- **Operational Cases** (`/cases/`): portfolio cases showing a problem, decision, verification criteria, and outcome.
- **Technology Evolution Map** (`/tech-map/`): an interactive account of historical relationships between technologies.

Each resource has one canonical URL. Static redirect pages preserve old links. Technical domains classify the writing; they do not drive the homepage UI.
